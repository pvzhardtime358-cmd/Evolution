Mod Information

Evolution reimagines PvZ2 experience by introducing fusion and upgrade mechanic. Combine your favorite plants to unlock powerful new forms.

  • Fusion and upgrade mechanics to power up plants
  • A brand-new, nostalgia-filled Memory Adventure World to explore
  • New classic zombies and lawns
  • Balanced gameplay that offers a fun, rewarding experience
  • New Glove Mechanic which can help fuse plants

Evolution is made for all PvZ fans who want to experience the classic nostalgia of the original game while discovering exciting new ways to play!
