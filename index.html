Mod Information

Evolution reimagines the PvZ2 experience by introducing a deep fusion and upgrade mechanic. Combine your favorite plants to unlock powerful new forms and build the ultimate defense.

  • Fusion and upgrade mechanics to power up your plants
  • A brand-new, nostalgia-filled PvZ1 World to explore
  • New classic zombies and lawns
  • Balanced gameplay that offers a fun, rewarding experience
  • Creative combinations that open up endless strategic possibilities

Evolution is made for all PvZ fans who want to experience the classic nostalgia of the original game while discovering exciting new ways to play!
